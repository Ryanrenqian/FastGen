## Hypothesis

Evaluating and exporting an exponential moving average of the DriftWorld
student with constant decay 0.999 reduces prediction flicker caused by
step-to-step parameter changes. EMA is checkpointed alongside the online
student. Optimization and training losses continue to use the online student.
